FAQ

  • The ASUS NUC Mini PC Ecosystem brings together AI-ready hardware, software toolkits, ISVs, and technology partners to build and deploy agentic edge AI solutions. It supports solution development, technical validation, commercialization, and global deployment.

  • The ecosystem is designed for ISVs developing AI applications and agents, as well as enterprises evaluating or deploying edge AI solutions. ASUS provides development resources, validated solutions, technical support, and partner connections for both groups.

  • ASUS NUC Mini PCs support AI applications across manufacturing, retail, healthcare, and general business use cases. Examples include local AI assistants, computer vision, content creation, data analysis, security, and other agentic AI workloads.

  • ASUS AI SuperBuild is a turnkey, on-premises AI platform for running large language models (LLMs) and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) locally. It helps organizations deploy local AI applications while keeping confidential data on the device for greater privacy and control.

  • ASUS AI SuperBuild currently supports Intel®-based ASUS NUC Mini PC platforms. It is optimized with Intel® OpenVINO™ to utilize the NPU and iGPU in Intel® Core™ Ultra processors for efficient local AI inference.

  • The ASUS Virtual Lab is a secure, cloud-hosted environment that provides remote access to the latest ASUS NUC platforms without requiring hardware procurement or shipping. Its preconfigured AI SDKs, drivers, development tools, and runtime environments help partners validate, optimize, and benchmark applications before deployment.

  • Eligible ISV partners may receive hardware samples, Virtual Lab access, technical support, joint solution development, co-marketing opportunities, and access to ASUS’s global sales network. Validated solutions may also be featured in ASUS enterprise solution catalogs.

  • Enterprises can contact ASUS with their business requirements and deployment goals. ASUS can help identify suitable NUC hardware, validated ISV applications, and ecosystem partners to create a tailored solution and support expansion across regions.
